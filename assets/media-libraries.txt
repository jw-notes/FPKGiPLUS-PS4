Libraries of FPKGi+'s trailer player
=====================================

FPKGi+ plays trailers with MPV and FFmpeg (source/platform/movie_mpv_ps4.cpp).
These libraries are statically linked into the app (eboot.bin), not into the
background downloader. The PS4 builds come from PacBrew's PS4 port packages
(https://github.com/PacBrew), the same builds JellyFin [PS] for PS4 uses. Each
library is under its own licence; the complete corresponding source is
available from the projects below. FPKGi+ as a whole is distributed under the
GNU GPL v3 (licenses/LICENSE.txt), which is compatible with each of them.
